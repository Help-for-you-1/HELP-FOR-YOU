Cancellation & Refund Policy

General Policy

Application submission does not guarantee approval or disbursement. Cancellation or refund requests will be reviewed according to the applicable service, transaction terms and applicable law.

Refund Eligibility

A refund may be considered where a customer has been charged for a transaction but the applicable service was not received, where a transaction has failed but the amount was debited, or where a refund is otherwise permitted under the applicable terms.

Cancellation

Customers who wish to cancel an eligible request should contact the authorized HELP FOR YOU team as soon as possible. Cancellation may not be possible after a service, transaction or applicable process has already been completed.

Refund Process

Customers should contact the authorized HELP FOR YOU team and provide the relevant application or transaction details. Approved refunds will normally be processed back to the original payment source used for the transaction.

Failed Transactions

If a payment fails or remains unsuccessful but the customer account is debited, the transaction will be checked and the applicable refund process will be initiated as required. Refunds may take approximately 5–21 days to reflect in the customer account depending on the payment method and banking process.

Non-Refundable or Completed Services

Where a service has already been provided, a transaction has been completed, or the applicable agreement does not permit cancellation or refund, the request may not qualify for a refund, subject to applicable law.
