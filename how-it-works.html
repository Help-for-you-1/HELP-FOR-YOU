How It Works

The HELP FOR YOU process is designed to keep application and loan information organized from application through authorized loan management.

01

Apply Now

Customer submits a loan application with the required information and documents.

02

Verification

Authorized staff or admin reviews the application and verifies the submitted information.

03

Loan Management

Approved loan and EMI details are managed through the appropriate authorized portal.
